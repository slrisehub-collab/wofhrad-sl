Who we are

Promoting justice and inclusive governance

WOFHRAD-SL is a non-governmental organisation founded in 2006 by a group of young women in Makeni — students of the University of Makeni inspired to be job creators, not job seekers. After the war ended in 2002, women in rural Sierra Leone still faced abuse and blocked access to justice in a male-dominated society.

We strive to ensure the promotion and protection of human rights and justice, democracy, and good governance — with women leading change in communities, districts and the nation.

Impact

Changing lives, changing policies

Two decades of advocacy — from rural safe spaces to the halls of parliament.

“After the 2018 elections, only 18 parliament members were female nationwide. In 2023, 41 female mandates were directly elected to parliament.”

The Gender Equality and Women's Empowerment Act (GEWE Act, 2022) now mandates 30% female representation.
