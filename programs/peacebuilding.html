Conflict Mitigation & Peace Building

Bombali, Karene, Freetown, Waterloo, Kono and Pujehun

Why we do this work:

  • Promote peaceful co-existence among stakeholders and political party supporters in six operational districts
  • Promote conflict-mitigation measures in those districts
  • Strengthen peace ambassadors to disseminate peaceful messages to the public

Field work spans Freetown Urban, the Northern districts, Kono and Pujehun in the South — face-to-face dialogue, not just workshops in the capital.
