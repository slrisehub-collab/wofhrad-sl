Gender Equality & Social Accountability

Co-funded by the EU and Trócaire

Overall objective: Civil society is strengthened to engage in women's empowerment and support women's active participation as actors in democratic governance and development in Sierra Leone.

Specific objective: Strengthen women's voice, influence and participation in democratic governance through a strengthened civil society.

What we deliver

  • Engagement & coordination — town hall meetings between duty-bearers and citizens on health, education, social protection and women's empowerment
  • Advocacy — 5,600 community members (2,800 women and 2,800 men), including persons with disabilities, reached with age-appropriate, gender-sensitive messages for peaceful elections and women's political participation
  • Community of Practice — district-level CoPs that coordinate and advocate for safe space and women's leadership
  • Young women in governance — 9 networks established across implementation districts, active in 14 operational wards
  • CSO effectiveness — accompaniment and capacity support so partners deliver milestones
