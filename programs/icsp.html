ICSP Women's Empowerment

Irish Civil Society Programme · Bombali District

The Irish Civil Society Programme (ICSP) works with women's networks in Bombali District to advance the rights of women in all spheres — including involvement in formal and informal decision-making, strengthening the capacity of women's networks, and holding duty bearers accountable for service delivery that captures the needs and aspirations of women.

Capacity is built through training in advocacy and leadership, and by creating linkages among networks and service providers. The project popularises gender-related acts and presses for their full implementation.

Bombali District Women Leadership Network

A registered membership network of Bombali women from different walks of life, sharing collective responsibility for improving the well-being of women and girls. The General Assembly is open to women from Bombali District regardless of political party.

How we empower women

  • Identify, mentor and coach aspiring women leaders
  • Advocate for women's and girls' participation in governance
  • Promote the rights of women, including persons with disabilities, especially in Bombali

Results

After the 2018 elections, only 18 parliament members were female nationwide. In 2023, 41 female mandates were directly elected. The GEWE Act of 2022 mandates 30% female representation in parliament and local councils.
