Girls Circle Collective

Out-of-school girls aged 9–18 · Bombali

The Girls Circle Collective (GCC), initiated in 2021, works with out-of-school girls so they are not left out of society but empowered. It is led by Purposeful, with five implementing partners nationwide: WOFHRAD (Bombali), CARL (Western Rural), MUNAFA (Karene), Dendembeh Federation (Falaba), and Pekin to Pekin (Moyamba & Bonthe).

Thematic areas: life skills, livelihood skills, safeguarding, literacy and numeracy, art, craft and poetry. Mentors and mentees meet in a safe space to listen to stories and share experience.

WOFHRAD's delivery

We establish and oversee 90 clubs / safe spaces, each led by a community-based mentor and accommodating 25 girls — more than 2,250 girls. Clubs are in 8 chiefdoms of Bombali: Ngowahun, Gbendembu, Mara, Safroko Limba, Makarie, Gbanti, Bombali Sieri and Bombali Sebora.

Staff monitor micro-grants that help girls start small businesses, check that mentors teach content on forced marriage, rape and menstrual care, and review reports sent through KoboToolbox.
