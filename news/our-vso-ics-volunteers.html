Our VSO-ICS Volunteers

07 August 2013

Here at WOFHRAD we have been lucky enough to have 2 volunteers working with us for 3 months. Abu Bakarr Conteh (a Sierra Leonean from Makeni, known to all as ABC) and Sophie Robbins (from the UK) were placed with our organisation as part of the International Citizen Service programme (ICS). The ICS programme is funded by the Department for Internation Development in the UK and is run by various NGO’s. Our volunteers came to us through VSO and it has been a great experience working with them.

Over the course of the 3 months we have done various activities such as court monitoring in Magburuka, organising the public lecture and marking essays in a writing competition. Sophie has helped us to edit our reports and concept notes and has been teaching ABC computer skills as they work. ABC and Sophie have also worked hard to create newsletter and this blog so that we can publicise our work not only in Makeni but around Sierra Leone and the world.

It has been very useful having the volunteers in the office and we have enjoyed working with them and also with VSO. We will be very sad to see Sophie leave on Saturday (this is in fact the volunteers last day at work) and will miss her a lot. Luckily for us ABC has asked if he can carry on volunteering for WOFHRAD after the end of the ICS programme. We are very pleased that he has enjoyed working with us so much that he wants to stay and we are very glad to have him.

We are hopeful that we will get two new VSO-ICS volunteers when the next group arrives in September.

We have also been lucky to have been approached by the National Youth Commision (NAYCOM), who have offered us the chance to have volunteers from within the country come and spend time working at WOFHRAD. This project is being run in conjunction with the UNDP and will be the topic of a later post.
